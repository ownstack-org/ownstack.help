Billing

OwnStack is paid for with prepaid usage credits. There are no subscriptions or plans. Servers themselves are billed by your cloud provider, and OwnStack never marks up infrastructure.

Usage credits

  • 500,000 credits = $1. Buy any amount from $1 to $50,000 on the Pricing page or with ownstack credits add 25.
  • Payment is by card through a Square checkout. We charge your card only when you buy credits; nothing renews.
  • Credits do not expire while your account is open.
  • Your apps keep running at a zero balance.
  • ownstack credits shows your balance and recent activity.

Credits are spent at the rates shown on the Pricing page. You get at least 30 days' email notice before usage starts drawing down your balance or before a rate goes up. The full terms are in the Billing Terms.

What your cloud provider bills

ItemWhere
EC2 / Compute Engine / VPS instance for each stackAWS / GCP / your provider
EBS / persistent diskYour provider
Outbound bandwidthYour provider
Backups (S3 storage)Your provider, if you configured stack backups to your bucket

Estimating monthly cost

For a single small Rails app on a shared t3.small:

  • EC2 t3.small on-demand: ~$15/mo
  • EBS 30 GiB gp3: ~$2.40/mo
  • Bandwidth (light): ~$1/mo
  • OwnStack: paid from your usage credits

Reserved instances or savings plans cut the EC2 cost by ~40%.

Invoices

Invoices, payment history and your payment method are on the Billing page of your dashboard. Questions about a charge go to support@ownstack.org.
